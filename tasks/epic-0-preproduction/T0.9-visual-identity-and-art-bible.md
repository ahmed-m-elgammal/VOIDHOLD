# T0.9 — Visual Identity + Art Bible

Owner: Art Director / visual lead. Reviewers: Game designer + Tech Artist + UI/UX.

## Goal

Create the visual system that makes VOIDHOLD feel authored, modern, and recognizable instead of like a generic AI-generated sci-fi asset collection.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Moodboard and reference board with explicit reasons for each reference.
- [ ] Shape-language sheet for habitat, industrial, utility, luxury, defense, and transport buildings.
- [ ] Palette: ice/snow/rock base, oxidized alloy, safety accents, warm habitation light, warning colors, and color-blind-safe alternatives.
- [ ] Material library with approved albedo, roughness, metallic, normal, AO, emissive, scale, and wear rules.
- [ ] Camera, orthographic scale, world-unit scale, fog, shadow, outline, and lighting standards.
- [ ] Character and enemy readability rules at gameplay zoom and portrait UI size.
- [ ] UI visual language: typography, cards, meters, icon grid, status hierarchy, motion, haptics, and sound pairing.
- [ ] “Do not use” sheet covering generic silhouettes, excessive bloom, noisy decals, random neon, texture soup, and unreviewed generated assets.

## Accept

- [ ] A finished hero building, unit, ground patch, enemy, and HUD mockup demonstrate the bible together.
- [ ] The scene is readable at low, medium, and high zoom on the minimum device target.
- [ ] Every visual rule has an owner and review checklist.
- [ ] Art provenance requirements are linked to T0.7.
