# T0.10 — Premium Vertical Slice Plan

Owner: Producer + Tech Lead + visual lead. Reviewer: QA.

## Goal

Prove the complete game experience and quality bar before scaling to all launch content.

## Slice scope

- One dome on one moon.
- Oxygen, habitat, greenhouse, food, solar, production, authority, and one flying enemy loop.
- Both colonist castes, carrierbot, placement, worker assignment, damage/repair, reports entry, tutorial entry, save/load, and resume.
- Production-quality environment kit, four or more finished buildings, final lighting direction, final UI direction, final SFX language, and portrait + landscape layouts.

## Gate

- [ ] First useful colony action within five minutes without external instructions.
- [ ] Touch placement, selection, camera, sheets, back hierarchy, and orientation switching feel deliberate.
- [ ] Save survives interruption, low storage simulation, app kill during write, and resume.
- [ ] Simulation and view remain synchronized after pause, speed change, and offline fast-forward.
- [ ] Minimum device reaches the agreed frame-time budget without sustained thermal failure.
- [ ] Visual lead rejects generic, repetitive, unreadable, or placeholder presentation.

## Decision

If the slice fails visual identity, mobile readability, save reliability, or minimum-device performance, full asset production pauses until the cause is fixed.
